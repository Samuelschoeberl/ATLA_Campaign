Avatar Spirit Check

Roll a d20 as part of a spirit-focused ritual or when prompted by the GM (typically at key story beats or after using a Spirit Stone).

- If the roll is even: you gain a number of temporary Spiritbending spellslots equal to the d20 result. These slots last until the end of the current scene or until spent, whichever comes first.
- If the roll is odd: you do not gain slots, but you receive one Spirit Echo — a small, narratively useful boon (example: advantage on one Spiritbending check, a single short vision providing a clue, or temporary insight on a spirit's motive). The GM chooses the precise echo based on context.

Rules & limits (assumptions — adjust with GM):

- Maximum gained slots cannot exceed the character's Bridge Level (or another GM-set cap).
- This check is usually available once per long rest unless a Spirit Stone or special ritual grants repeated checks.
- Spirit slots gained are of the lowest available Spiritbending tier and must be used for Spiritbending moves or effects.

Example:

- You roll a d20 and get 14 (even). You gain 14 temporary Spiritbending spellslots (subject to the GM cap). You spend 2 of them to cast a Spirit Ward and keep the rest until the end of the scene.

Spiritbending Moves

Spiritbending moves are special techniques that use the Avatar Spirit's connection to the Spirit World. They tend to be more narrative and rarely purely destructive — instead focusing on influence, protection, communication, and spiritual transformation.

Core contract:

- Inputs: Spiritbending slots (from Bridge Level, Spirit Stones, or Avatar Spirit Checks).
- Outputs: A move effect (see moves below).
- Failure modes: On a failed GM opposed roll or failed concentration, some spirit effects can backfire or produce an unintended Spirit Echo.

Sample Moves

1. Spirit Ward (cost: 2 slots)

- Effect: Create a protective aura that grants allies within 3m + (1m × slots used) advantage on Spirit-related saves for X minutes.

2. Soul Sight (cost: 1 slot)

- Effect: You and one ally can see invisible spirits or spirit effects for 1 minute. Provides a single insight on a spirit's nature.

3. Calm of Ages (cost: 3 slots)

- Effect: Attempt to pacify a hostile spirit or calm an area. Target makes a WIS save vs DC = 8 + slots used; on failure the spirit is pacified for 1d6 minutes.

4. Spirit Relay (cost: variable)

- Effect: Spend slots to send a short message into the Spirit World (or receive one). Each slot adds clarity and range.

5. Ancestral Echo (cost: 4 slots)

- Effect: Temporarily call on an ancestor's skill — grant an ally a one-time automatic success on a non-combat skill check tied to the ancestor's expertise.

Design notes:

- Moves should be negotiated with the GM for balance and narrative fit.
- Spiritbending is more flexible than elemental bending: creativity and roleplay matter.
