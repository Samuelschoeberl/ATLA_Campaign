# Avatar Spirit Check

Roll a d20 as part of a spirit-focused ritual or when prompted by the GM (typically at key story beats or after using a Spirit Stone).

- If the roll is even: you gain a number of temporary [[Spiritbending]] spellslots equal to the d20 result. These slots last until the end of the current scene or until spent, whichever comes first.
- If the roll is odd: you do not gain slots, but you receive one _Spirit Echo_ — a small, narratively useful boon (example: advantage on one Spiritbending check, a single short vision providing a clue, or temporary insight on a spirit's motive). The GM chooses the precise echo based on context.

Rules & limits (assumptions — adjust with GM):

- Maximum gained slots cannot exceed the character's Bridge Level (or another GM-set cap).
- This check is usually available once per long rest unless a Spirit Stone or special ritual grants repeated checks.
- Spirit slots gained are of the lowest available Spiritbending tier and must be used for Spiritbending moves or effects.

Example:

- You roll a d20 and get 14 (even). You gain 14 temporary Spiritbending spellslots (subject to the GM cap). You spend 2 of them to cast a Spirit Ward and keep the rest until the end of the scene.
