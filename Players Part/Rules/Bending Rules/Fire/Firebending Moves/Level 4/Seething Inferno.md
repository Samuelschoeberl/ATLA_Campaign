[[Action]]
[[Level]]: 4


- Range: Zone / Area
- **Duration:** Instant
- Description: Unleash a wide wave of fire. You may spend up to [[Firebending DC]] Stress to boost the effect.

**Effects:**

- Use: Each stress spent adds 5d6 fire damage to the attack.
- Roll: [[Attack Roll]].
- On a 10+: All targets in the area take base damage plus the extra dice from stress spent.
- On a 7–9: Succeed with a cost—choose one: half the area, one less damage die, or gain +1 Stress.
- On a miss (6 or less): You lose control; gain +2 Stress and the GM narrates a costly complication.
