Firebenders draw power from passion, heat, and intensity. Stress is both a resource and a danger: it fuels more powerful techniques, but too much leaves a bender unstable and at risk of losing control. The following rules convert narrative stress into a simple mechanical resource for firebending moves.

When do you gain [[Stress Level]]:
- For every 10 damage you take you take 1 [[Stress Level]]
- For every [[Firebending Slot]] used in a [[Damaging Move]]
When you loose [[Stress Level]]:
- 1 [[Stress Level]] after every turn
