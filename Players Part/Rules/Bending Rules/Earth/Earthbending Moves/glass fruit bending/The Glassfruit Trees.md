### 🍇 Origin: 

High on the outer cliffs of Omashu and the surrounding valley grow the rare **Glassfruit Trees**—bizarre plants that draw heavy mineral content from the stone and soil, producing **glass-like fruit** with dense crystalline seeds. These seeds:


- Grow in **five known varieties**, each with distinct elemental-reactive properties.
    
- Can be manipulated by Earthbenders with precise control, refined through spiritual discipline.
    

Local legend says the **Earth Lion Turtle** left these trees here, believing their presence would help the people **grow with stone, not just build from it**.

---

**Practiced by:** Earthbenders of Omashu, the secretive **Order of the Glassfruit**  
**Core Techniques:**

Glassbenders use **Glassfruit Seeds** as the basis of their fighting and crafting style. Unlike traditional Earthbending, this form is:

- **Finer**, with **smaller, more precise motions than usual Earthbending .
    
- Often done with **bare hands or finger movements**, not stances.
    
- Focused on **resonance, fracturing, refraction**, and **vibrational manipulation**.
    

---

### 🌈 The Five Seeds of the Glassfruit:

Each seed color has unique applications:

1. **Red Flameglass**
    
    - Slightly combustible. Ignites under pressure or heat.
        
    - Used in explosive traps, ignited whips, or forged blades.
        
    - **Enhanced by Firebenders.**
        
2. **Blue Soundstone**
    
    - Vibrates and hums when bent.
        
    - Used in sound manipulation, echolocation, sonic shockwaves.
        
    - **Enhanced by Airbenders.**
        
3. **Green Bramblecrystal**
    
    - Fractal growth pattern. Rapidly expands under water or chi.
        
    - Used for entrapment, growing crystal armor, or climbing gear.
        
    - **Enhanced by Waterbenders.**
        
4. **Yellow Flashcore**
    
    - Emits blinding pulses under friction or chi stimulation.
        
    - Used in stunning bursts, signal flares, or reflective barriers.
        
    - **Enhanced by Light-reflective surfaces or Sun Chi.**
        
5. **Clear Soulseed**
    
    - Rare. Used for **resonance harmonization** and **spiritual bending**.
        
    - Used in **rituals**, **spirit communication**, and **healing constructs**.
        
    - **Only effective when paired with a Resonance Stone.**
        

---

### 🔥 The Crystalbenders

A secret sect called the **Order of the Crystalfang** lives beneath Omashu’s outer wall in vast, glass-tiled tunnels. They are:

- A **multibender alliance**: Earthbenders, Firebenders, Airbenders working in tandem.
    
- Focused on refining Glassfruit Seeds into **glass weapons, armor, instruments**, and **ritual relics**.
    
- Trained in **team bending maneuvers**, using Fire to heat and shape, Air to cool and vibrate, and Earth to control and fracture.
    

They believe the art of Glassbending is **a gift of the Lion Turtle**, and they **worship a large Resonance Stone** known as:

> **“The Heart of Clarity”**

This **Relic** is deeply attuned to spirit energy and plays tones that only Crystalfangs can interpret. Some say it **sings** when the right soul is nearby.

---

### 🌀 Resonance Stone & the Party:

The **Heart of Clarity** is actually **one of several Resonance Stones**—ancient spiritual relics tied to the slumbering Lion Turtle.

The party **must obtain or attune to it** to:

- Unlock advanced spiritual techniques.
    
- Amplify their elemental abilities in resonance with the world.
    
- Possibly **awaken latent powers** or allow **inter-elemental harmony**.
    

---

### 🧩 Hooks:

- A Crystalfang initiate seeks the party's help after the Heart of Clarity **goes silent** for the first time in generations.
    
- The Order is being **manipulated by a rogue faction** of Stonebloods who want to **use the Resonance Stone to awaken the Lion Turtle**.
    
- Spirit-corrupted Glassfruit trees begin growing in unnatural ways—hinting at a **breach from the Spirit World**.
