## Glassfruit Team-Up Techniques 

### X = y*z

-  are the number of **bending slots** contributed by each bender.
    
- This rewards **balanced team effort** over just one person dumping points.
    
-`X = y × z`.
    

> **Example:**
> 
> - Earthbender contributes 2 slots (y = 2)
>     
> - Firebender contributes 3 (z = 3)
>     → `X = 2 × 3 = 6`


These are **powerful, multi-element, terrain-altering** moves that consume Glassfruit Seeds. Each scales based on X and has different effects depending on the seed used.

---

### 🔴 **Inferno Bloom (Red Flamegrape)**

Must be initiated by a Firebender in line of sight

> A cluster of buried Flame-glass shards detonates in a chain [[Reaction]].

- **Effect:** Explosion in an area, dealing **Xd6  bludgeoning + half fire damage**
    
- **Radius:** 2  meter * X
    
- **[[Lingering Effect]]**
    

---

### 💨**Resonant Collapse (Blue Soundbanana)**
Must be initiated by a Airbender in line of sight 

> A soundblast builds from multiple frequencies and shatters the ground.

- **Effect:** Targets in area must make CON save of X*2 or take **Xd8 thunder damage** and fall prone
    
- **Secondary:** Earth terrain in the zone becomes **difficult terrain**
    
- **Radius:**  2*X meter
    
- **Special:** If X ≥ 12, causes **localized landslide** or ceiling collapse in enclosed space
    
- **Narrative Use:** Clear out guards, knock down spirit constructs
    

---

### 🔵 **BlueBerry Glass Surge**
Must be initiated by a Waterbender in line of sight

>  Stick and grow trapping enemies and reshaping the terrain.

- **Effect:** Targets in area must make  a DCX * 2 Dex save to jump aside or  get Immobilised in cold soft and barely liquid Glass.
    
- **Radius:** X * 2
    
- **Duration:**  X minutes but trapped creatures can as an [[Action]] make a strength saving throws to break out with the same DC - Rounds since getting trapped
    
- **Special:** If X ≥ 8, brambles regenerate once if destroyed
    
- **Narrative Use:** Reshape battlefield, trap beasts or enemy squads
    

---

### 🟡 **FlashLemon Refractor Beam 
Must be initiated by a Firebender in line of sight

> Focused light is amplified by mirrored shards into a blinding beam.
- **Effect:** Targets in area must make  a DCX * 2 Int save to shield or close their eyes or be Blinded for one turn
Range:  10 * X meter cone
- **Bonus:** Deal **Xd8 radiant damage** to one target hit directly
    
- **Lasting Effect:** All affected enemies suffer _disadvantage on perception_ for 4 turns
    
- **Special:** If X ≥ 10, the Focused damage is doubled 
    


---

### ⚪ **Soulseed Harmonic Seal (Clear Soulseed)**
Must be initiated by another Earthbender in line of sight
> A ritual resonance of GlassSeeds pressed on the Ground around the earthbenders 
   [[Concentration]] for both
- **Effect:** As the air vibrates with a cleansing energy as creatures of your choice within radius gain 10 * X [[General Armor]]

- **Radius:** within line of sight up to 100 meter
    
- **Duration:** Lasts until stopped concentrating or for 1 hour.
    
- **Special:** If X ≥ 15, the seal becomes **permanent** unless shattered by spiritual corruption
    
- **Narrative Use:** Close spirit portals, bind corrupted spirits, defend a holy site
