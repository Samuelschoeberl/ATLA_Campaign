Avatar TTRPG — Session 6
Where we left off

Party is in Misty Palms Oasis investigating the exploded distillery and a newly exposed basement chamber full of ancient fish-pictograph scrolls about Dīnu, the oasis’s invisible goldfish spirit (appears only when you aren’t looking).

A dropped potion formed a watery portal. A hand reached through; the party pulled back Ash, who’d been on a long spirit-world journey (felt like ~45 years to Ash).

Key events

Hidden archive: Scrolls describe Dīnu phasing between Spirit and Material Worlds; only Dīnu’s poop manifests materially. Verses hint you must bring all four elements into harmony to contact Dīnu.

Fire-pets & zodiac quest: The muscular young firebender continues bonding with animals (fire cat, Woofie the fire dog, Cuckoo the fire rooster) and wants connections to all 12 zodiac animals (today’s attempt at a fire rat ended… poorly).

Cat chaos: The fire cat singed some scrolls before being dismissed.

Stabilizing the chamber: A richly dressed, highly skilled earthbender arrives—revealed as the King of Omashu. Using a resonant glass pomegranate technique, he and the party stabilize the collapsing room (massive group healing/soothing resonance).

Omashu hook: The king, an acquaintance of Vin Kotak (the tense airbender from earlier sessions), invites the party to Omashu to learn more about resonance stones and how the party uniquely uses them.

Spirit contact via poop ritual: The group combined five poop pellets with the four elements (watered, frozen, sand-encapsulated, burned, air-swirled).

Result: a thick, spiritually potent smoke.

Ash, using a spirit-sight technique (“Si U Li”), saw and heard Dīnu emerge and briefly speak; others only heard a faint “blub.” Dīnu then faded; the smoke dissipated and the pellets were consumed.

Wrap-up: Bath at the oasis; plans made to travel north to Omashu next session, following the king’s lead and the party’s prior tip that a resonance stone lies there.

What we learned

Dīnu’s nature: Invisible by choice; only poop is material; contact likely requires balanced multi-element ritual.

Resonance stones: Can imbue/awaken secondary elements (party has experienced this). The king can teach more in Omashu.

Spirit access: Spirit-sight lets mortals perceive otherwise invisible spirit effects, at least briefly.
