Session 5 Transcript

Spirit Snake Scale:

Party found a spirit snake scale on the ground.

It can be used as a reaction to flash and impose disadvantage.


Milking Antics:

The group tried to milk desert oxen to mix with cactus juice for cocktails.

Multiple failed Nature checks led to confusion over whether the ox was male or female (“Am I holding a penis?!”).

They eventually gave up on proper milking but still mixed cactus juice with mystery milk for later.


Journey to Tapi’s Village:

Party headed toward Tapi’s home village near an oasis.

Traversed a rocky descent, using Earthbending to create steps and platforms (some attempts failed hilariously).


Caravan & Hyena Falcon Encounter:

They spotted a merchant caravan (camels carrying barrels of cactus juice).

The caravan seemed fleeing something but let the group catch up.

Hyena falcons attacked.

The party used creative bending — including launching a flaming barrel — to drive the falcons off.


Arrival at Tapi’s Village:

They arrived at Tapi’s tourist-friendly oasis village, known for its premium cactus juice.

Tapi’s house: described as bohemian and cluttered, filled with shiny decorations, bottles, and jars.

They met Tapi’s mom (a warm, hippie-like woman). She mentioned that Tapi’s dad was out on the cactus farm.


The Oasis & Temple:

The group visited the oasis temple, which had:

Drawings and statues of a fish spirit — but none of them looked the same.

Priestess Ban Ban, who explained:

The fish spirit disappeared a few days ago.

It used to communicate with her via bubbles.

She has drunk the holy water herself.



Mahogany dove into the pool, searching for signs of the spirit.

She felt faint traces of spiritual energy, but nothing alive.

Instead, she found poop, which she proudly brought up for examination.


The group speculated:

The spirit may have been taken, changed form, or left through an unseen passage.

Some PCs (esp. Puy) rehashed the ongoing argument about reselling cactus juice as “holy juice.”



Evening at Tapi’s Home:

Tapi’s mom suddenly burst in dramatically announcing Tapi’s dad had an accident.

Turns out he had just fallen into a cactus.

Anju helped him, and he was fine.

They then relaxed, enjoying:

Cucumber sandwiches and watermelon-quinoa salad.

Cactus-milk cocktails (surprisingly good, rated 9/10).

Cactus juice — some PCs got drunk after failing Con saves.



Cliffhanger:

Just as everyone was comfortably drunk and chilling, they heard strange noises outside near the farm — something is happening.



---

Session Summary:

The group picked up a spirit snake scale and concocted a strange but tasty cactus-milk cocktail after a chaotic ox-milking attempt. On their way to Tapi’s home village, they helped a merchant caravan fend off hyena falcons with creative bending (including a flaming barrel).

At the oasis temple, they learned from priestess Ban Ban that the fish spirit who once communicated through bubbles had disappeared. Mahogany dove into the pool, sensing faint spiritual energy but finding only poop — perhaps a remnant of the missing spirit.

Returning to Tapi’s house, the party met his dramatic mother, who announced his father’s “terrible accident” (which turned out to be just a cactus fall). After Anju helped him, they spent the evening enjoying cocktails, food, and cactus juice — some drinking heavily.

The night ended with a cliffhanger: suspicious noises outside near the cactus farm.
