- Campaign recap: In Tapioca’s hometown (Misty Palm Oasis), the party learned to use spirit goldfish poop by combining the four elements to create a spiritual “smoke” they could see through with spirit sight, revealing a goldfish spirit that gave clues.
    
- They previously met the King of Omashu, who warned about internal conflict in the Lotus: the Nightbloom faction resists the Lotus’s plan to find/train a new Avatar. The King asked them to meet him when they reach Omashu.
    
- Current scene: The party is a few hours into a desert trek toward Omashu (with enough supplies this time). They spot three air nomads (two adults + a child) who glide down and ask for help: bandits stole their pet spirit, “Spikeberry,” a fluffy hedgehog spirit that grows berries on its back; the berries have turned bitter, suggesting it may be sick.
    
- The party agrees to help, scouts a bandit camp (about 5–6 bandits), and plans a distraction (mist/fog). A scouting dragon/lemur recon goes a bit wrong and combat starts.
    
- Fight highlights: bandits include benders (notably fire/earth/water). The party uses mist, fire gets redirected, the waterbender villain gets taken down, cages are cut open, and the party rescues the stolen spirits (their dragon + Spikeberry). They capture at least one surviving bandit.
    
- After the fight they release other captured animals. Spikeberry seems unwell/imbalanced spiritually, and the party decides they should get it to Omashu for help.
