
The party met in Sandlocke City for the Ritual of Ressonance for finding the avatar, since it detects spiritual energy. They get to know each other and some high-ranking officials of the Lotus Organisation. Most of the party members went there because they are bending prodigies.

They sleep at the Temple of the Ritual, where they get surprised by Ula, a young waterbender from an underground village. They travel with her underground to save their home frog spirit. When they tried to heal the spirit's corruption it attacks them and when defeating it, it calms down. The spirit sent Ash to the spirit plane to train spiritual techniques.

The following day of the Ritual, The Nightbloom attacks. During the fight, the party absorbs the Resonance Stone. The leaders of the Lotus chase off the Nightbloom members. Phot the Sandsnake, Puy's teacher, gives them the quest to go to Omashu and find another Resonance Stone.

On their way, they stop at the Si Wong rock and discover the village on top of it. They encounter a spirit that had always defended the village, but now is getting corrupted and it attacks them. The party defeats it because Mahogany absorbs its spiritual energy and unlocks a new spiritual form.
