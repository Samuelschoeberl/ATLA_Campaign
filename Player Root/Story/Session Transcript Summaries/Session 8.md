- After traveling to a mountain top village with a monastry, the group approaches Spikeberry, the hedgehog spirit, trying to comfort it. It seems feverish, distressed, and lashes out defensively, lightly injuring a party member.
    
- Spikeberry suddenly corrupts and grows, transforming into a large, dark, hostile version of itself. The berries on its back mutate into spiked, thorny “bad blueberries.” Initiative is rolled.
    
- During combat:
    
    - One character panic–shape-shifts into a snake-elephant hybrid, creating a defensive aura that grants nearby allies extra armor.
        
    - The party tries precision attacks, slicing off corrupted berries and throwing them back.
        
    - Waterbending is used to freeze the ground, but the corrupted spirit breaks free.
        
    - Spikeberry attacks with whipping berry tendrils, dealing heavy bludgeoning damage.
        
    
- The fight destabilizes the area. Spikeberry burrows into the mountain and escapes, leaving behind massive structural damage.
    
- The temple begins collapsing as a key sandstone support breaks.
    
- The party escapes the temple just in time and sees a spiritual energy orb/pearl—the temple’s core—roll down the mountainside, setting up the next session’s objective: saving or recovering the spirit core.
