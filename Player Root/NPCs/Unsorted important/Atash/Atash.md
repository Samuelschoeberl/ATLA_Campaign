The Last Avatar.
From Sandlocke City. 
Atash, the last Avatar and leader of the desert tribe, defended his people from a colossal air spirit that swept in from beyond the seas, tearing tents and scattering herds. Drawing on earth, stone, and sand, he raised immovable walls and churned rivers of dunes to blind and drive the entity back. He prevailed—but at the cost of his life. His sacrifice saved the tribe, and his lineage endures quietly among them.
