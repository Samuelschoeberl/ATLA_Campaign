#Action 
#Concentration 

- Range: [[water]] meters. 

**Effect**:
You attempt to Disturb a Spirit to make it turn into its dark volatile form.
If the Move fails in any way the Spirit cannot be enraged this way anymore for [[Water_charge]] turns.

The Spirit rolls a Wisdom Saving throw against your [[Waterbending DC]]. 
	On Success the Move Fails.
- On a Failure:
	- The spirit becomes [[Immobilised]]
	- You have to stay within range until the start of your next turn or the Move fails.
	- Roll [[Water_charge]] d8.
	- If you rolled higher than the spirits current Hitpoints it gains [[water]] [[chaos_energy]].
