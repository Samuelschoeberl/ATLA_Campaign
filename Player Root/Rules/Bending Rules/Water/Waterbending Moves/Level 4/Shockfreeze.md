#### Shockfreeze (Cantrip)**

- **Cost:** None.
- **Type:** [[Bonus Action]].
- **Range:** The same as the Waterbending used as [[Action]] f
- **Effect:**  
    After using an [[Action]] to use a Waterbending Move that consumes water charges you can use your [[Bonus Action]] to let every creature hit by your [[Action]] roll a Strength Saving throw against your [[Player Root/Rules/Bending Rules/Water/Mechanics/Waterbending DC]] or become [[Immobilised]].
