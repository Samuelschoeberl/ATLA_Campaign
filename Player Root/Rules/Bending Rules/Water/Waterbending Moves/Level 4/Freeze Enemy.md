#Reaction 

- **Range:**  [[Water]] Level / 2 meters.

- The waterbender can attempt to freeze a creature that has been hit by a Move that is using [[Water_charge]](s). It has to succeed a Strength Saving throw against your [[Waterbending DC]] or become [[Paralysed]]. 
- The target gets to repeat its save at the start of its turn. The DC drops by 1 every round.
