#Action 
#Concentration 

- Range: [[water]] meters. 

**Effect**:
You attempt to soothe a Spirit.
If the Move fails in any way the Spirit is enraged more and cannot be soothed this way anymore for [[Water_charge]] turns.

The Spirit rolls a Wisdom Saving throw against your [[Waterbending DC]]. 
	On Success the Move Fails.
- On a Failure:
	- The spirit becomes [[Immobilised]]
	- If the spirit takes Damage the Move fails.
	- You have to stay within range until the start of your next turn or the Move fails.
	- Roll [[Water_charge]] d8.
	- If you rolled higher than the spirits current Hitpoints it calms down and either returns to the Spiritworld or turns into its non hostile form. If you roll Lower than the spirits Hitpoints the Move fails.
