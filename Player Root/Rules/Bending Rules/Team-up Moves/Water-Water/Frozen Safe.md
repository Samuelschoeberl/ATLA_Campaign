#Reaction (waterbender)
 
You may use Levels from **Water only**.
Each Bender-decides to which pool to add their Bending slots (min 1 in each):
-  **X**: X \* 10 [[Barrier]] and X \* 20 [[Fire Armor]] until the start if the Targets Turn
-  **Y**: Y \* 5 Healing at the start of the Targets turn if barrier remains unbroken
Range: X + Y from all benders
- If the barrier is not destroyed at the **start of the Targets next turn**, as the ice thaws, they are healed but their **[[Movement]] is halved**.

- Encase an ally in a pillar of glacial ice with a liquid healing core.
