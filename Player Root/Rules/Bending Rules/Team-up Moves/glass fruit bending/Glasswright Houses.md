Glasswright Houses

Small, specialized guilds that manage Glassfruit cultivation, annealing and shaping in and around Omashu.

- Typical composition: Earthbender (substrate & lattice), Waterbender (hydration & pressure regulation), Firebender (annealing), Airbender (shaping). Some Houses add spiritbenders or smiths.

- Role in the setting: They run terraces, operate pressure cradles, and compete through signature press profiles. Houses keep secret techniques that produce distinct glassware and battlefield seed-effects.

Tags: #glasswright #guild #omashu #glassfruit #craft #player_root

Quick storytelling hook (skillcheck):

- Situation: A House's annealing fails mid-press and a half-formed crop threatens to explode.
- Skillcheck: Firebender makes a Press Roll (DC = 10 + X) to stabilise heat; Waterbender may make a Pressure Check (DC = 8 + floor(X/2)) to reduce steam. Success: crop tempered; failure: minor shard/steam blast and damaged press. Use failure consequences as an encounter or obstacle.
