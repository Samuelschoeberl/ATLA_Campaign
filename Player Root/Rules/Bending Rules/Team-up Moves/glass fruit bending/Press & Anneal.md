Press & Anneal

The controlled heating and pressurised annealing stage used to strengthen Glassfruit and activate elemental traits.

Core rule summary:

- Typical initiators: Fire + Water (team-up). Use X = product of contributing slots to scale difficulty.
- Base DC: 8 + X (adjust per GM). Firebender usually makes the primary roll; Waterbender may assist for advantage or to reduce DC.

Outcomes:

- Success: fruit is "Tempered" (see Tempered (trait)).
- Minor fail: reduced HP, minor steam/shard hazard.
- Critical fail: explosive fracture (area hazard, loot lost).

Tags: #press #anneal #mechanics #glassfruit #omashu

Storytelling hook (skillcheck):

- A rival House sabotaged a pressure valve; the team must reroute steam while holding the anneal. Firebender Press Roll (DC 12 + X/2) to keep temp steady; Waterbender Pressure Check (DC 10) to vent safely. Failure escalates into an environmental hazard encounter.
