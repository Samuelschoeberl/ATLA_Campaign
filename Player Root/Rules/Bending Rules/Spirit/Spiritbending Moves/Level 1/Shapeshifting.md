#transformation

- Range: [[Self]]
- **Duration:** varies
	- Whenever you loose your shapeshifting form involuntarily (by getting reduced to 0 HP in that form) you roll a Wisdom Saving Throw against your own [[Spiritbending DC]] or you gain [[spirit]] [[chaos_energy]].
- Effect: 
	- You transform into a creature using [[Spiritbending Slot]]. 1 Spiritbending slot = 1 Transformation Point (TP)
