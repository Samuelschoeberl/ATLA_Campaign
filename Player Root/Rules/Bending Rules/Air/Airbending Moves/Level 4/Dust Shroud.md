#Action

-**Cost:** 5 [[Airbending_slot]]
- **Range:** 10 meters, single target

**Effect:** A spiraling shroud of grit slams into the target. They must make a Constitution save versus your [[Airbending DC]]. On a failure, the target is **Blinded** until the end of its next turn. On a success, the dust stings but vision clears immediately.
