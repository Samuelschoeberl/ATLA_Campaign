#Action

- **Range:** 2 * [[firebending_slot]] meters radius centered on a point you can see.
- **Duration:** [[firebending_slot]] rounds. Concentration.
- Shape a fire-pig that devours hostile flames and leaves glowing coals behind.
- Enemy fire effects in the area are suppressed while you concentrate unless their creator succeeds on a bending contest against your [[Firebending DC]].
- Creatures of your choice that start their turn in the area gain [[firebending_slot]] d8 [[Barrier]].
- Creatures not chosen by you take [[firebending_slot]] * 2d8 fire when they start their turn in the area.
