- **Range:** Self.
- **Duration:** Until the start of your next turn.
- Shape a fire-rabbit around your feet and move up to 2 * [[firebending_slot]] meters without provoking reactions from creatures you can see.
- Until the duration ends, the first attack against you has disadvantage.
- If that attack misses, the attacker takes [[firebending_slot]] * 2d6 fire.
