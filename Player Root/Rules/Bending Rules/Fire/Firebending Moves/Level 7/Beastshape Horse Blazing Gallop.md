#Action

- **Range:** Self.
- **Duration:** [[firebending_slot]] rounds. Concentration.
- Shape a fire-horse beneath you or an ally within 1 meter.
- The rider gains 3 * [[firebending_slot]] meters Movement and ignores difficult terrain caused by fire, ash, smoke, or loose ground.
- Once during the duration, the rider may trample through creatures in a straight line up to 3 * [[firebending_slot]] meters.
- **Damage:** [[firebending_slot]] * 3d8 fire.
- **Save:** Dex save against your [[Firebending DC]] for half damage.
