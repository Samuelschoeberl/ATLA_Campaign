Smoldering Focus

[[Utility / Support]]
[[Level]]: 1
[[Additional attributes]]: Stress-aware

- Range: [[Self]]
- **Duration:** Instant
- Description: Calm your breath and focus heat into a small, steady kernel for control or precision.

**Effects:**

- Roll: Roll +firebending.
- On a 10+: Reduce your Stress by 1. Choose one: +1 forward to your next firebending roll, or suppress a small fire for 1 minute.
- On a 7–9: No stress change; gain +1 forward to your next firebending roll.
- On a miss (6 or less): Gain +1 Stress and the GM introduces a small complication.
