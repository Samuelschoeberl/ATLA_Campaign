#Concentration

- **Range:** 5 * [[firebending_slot]] meters.
- **Duration:** [[firebending_slot]] rounds. Concentration.
- Shape a living fire-dragon, unstable but real.
- When summoned, choose a point in range. Creatures of your choice within 2 * [[firebending_slot]] meters of that point make a Dex save against your [[Firebending DC]].
- **Damage:** [[firebending_slot]] * 5d10 fire on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
- While you concentrate, you may use a Bonus Action on each of your turns to move the dragon up to 3 * [[firebending_slot]] meters and make one bite against a creature within 2 meters of it.
- [[Fire Attack Roll]]
- **Bite Damage:** [[firebending_slot]] * 3d10 fire.
- If your concentration breaks because you take damage, the dragon collapses into a 2 * [[firebending_slot]] meter burst centered on itself. All creatures in the burst, including allies, make a Dex save against your [[Firebending DC]] or take [[firebending_slot]] * 3d10 fire.
