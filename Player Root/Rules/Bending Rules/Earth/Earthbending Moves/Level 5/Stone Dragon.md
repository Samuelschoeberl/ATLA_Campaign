**Stone Dragon**

**Level 5 Slot**
- Animate a massive serpentine construct of earth and stone.
- Range: 5 * [[Earthbending slot]] meters
- Duration: 1 minute
#Concentration

**Effect**:
Create a dragon-like construct of stone that follows your commands. The Stone Dragon has [[Earthbending slot]] * 10 [[Hitpoints]], can move [[Earthbending slot]] * 3 meters per turn, and can make attacks using your [[Earth Attack Roll]] dealing [[Earthbending slot]]d12 bludgeoning damage. You can command it as a [[Bonus Action]].

**Usage**: Powerful summoning ability for sustained combat support and battlefield presence.
