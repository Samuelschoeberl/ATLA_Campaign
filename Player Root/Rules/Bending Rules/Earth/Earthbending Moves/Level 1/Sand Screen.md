#Bonus_Action 

- **Range:** Self (2 meters)
- **Duration:** 1 Round

**Effects:**
- You flick a burst of fine sand upward, forming a protective screen.
- Gain [[Earthbending slot]] d4 [[Player Root/variable/secondary_stat/Evasion|Evasion]] against ranged attacks.
- If an attack misses you by 3 or less, you may choose to obscure the attacker, imposing disadvantage on their next attack.
