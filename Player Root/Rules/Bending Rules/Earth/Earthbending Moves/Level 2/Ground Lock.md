
#Bonus_Action  

- Range: 2 * [[Earthbending slot]] Meters
- Duration: Until Saved.
- Save: Dex Save against your [[Earthbending DC]].
- Target: 1 * [[Earthbending slot]]  Enemies within range with ground contact
- The Target becomes [[Immobilised]].
- They get to roll a str Save against your [[Earthbending DC]]at the end of their turn.
