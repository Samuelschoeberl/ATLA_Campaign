#Reaction

- **Range:** [[Melee]]
- **Trigger:** You or an adjacent ally is targeted by a melee attack.

**Effects:**
- You sweep sand in an arc, softening the blow.
- Reduce the incoming damage by [[Earthbending slot]] d6 
- If the attack gets absorbed completly the target must succeed an Intelligence saving throw against your [[Earthbending DC]] or the next attack on it is made with advantage.
