#Action

- Range: 3 * [[Earthbending slot]] Meters
- [[Earth Attack Roll]]
- Damage: [[Earthbending slot]]d8 bludgeoning damage
- Save: Strength Save against your [[Earthbending DC]]

**Effect**:
Command the earth to form a grasping hand that restrains a target. On a hit, the target takes damage and must make a Strength save or become [[Player Root/Rules/core rules/Conditions/Restrained]]. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns.
