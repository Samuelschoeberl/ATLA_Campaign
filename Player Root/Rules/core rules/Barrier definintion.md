Whenever you suffer unblocked damage [[Barrier]] halves (rounded down)

For example when you have 15 [[Barrier]] and suffer 18 damage you suffer 3 Damage and [[Barrier]]  halves to 7. 

Barrier Damage reduction is always applied before any other (e.g. [[General Armor]]) (yes the barrier also halves if the remaining damage is blocked)
