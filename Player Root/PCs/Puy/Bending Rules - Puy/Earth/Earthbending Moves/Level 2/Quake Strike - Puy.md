#Action_Puy

- Slam the ground to create localized tremors.
- Range: [[Earthbending slot]] (6) meter radius centered on you
- Damage: [[Earthbending slot]] (6)d8 bludgeoning damage
- Save: Dexterity Save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (7)

**Effect**: All creatures within range must make a Dexterity save or take full damage and be knocked Prone. On a successful save, they take half damage and remain standing.
