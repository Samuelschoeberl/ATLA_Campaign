**Gale Vault**

**Level 3 Slot**

- Launch yourself on a column of air up to 5*[[Airbending_slot]] (30) meter to an unoccupied space you can see.
- Creatures within [[Airbending_slot]] (30) m of your landing spot must succeed on a STR save or be knocked prone. On a success or failure, they are pushed  [[Airbending_slot]] (30) meter away from you.
- If you pass over difficult terrain or hazards, you ignore them during the vault.
