#Action_Sora

- **Range:** 4 * [[firebending_slot]] (9) meters.
- **Duration:** [[firebending_slot]] (9) rounds. Concentration.
- Create a tiny fire-rat that runs along the ground, under furniture, and through narrow gaps.
- While it lasts, you can see through the fire-rat's ember eyes if it remains within range.
- As part of the Action, or on a later turn as a Bonus Action, you may detonate it.
- **Damage:** [[firebending_slot]] (9) * 2d6 fire in a 1 meter radius.
- **Save:** Dex save against your [[Firebending DC]] (9) for half damage.
