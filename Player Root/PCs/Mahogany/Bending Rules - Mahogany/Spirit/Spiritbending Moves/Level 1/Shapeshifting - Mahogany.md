- Range: [[Self]] (0)
- **Duration:** varies
	- Whenever you loose your shapeshifting form involuntarily (by getting reduced to 0 HP in that form) you roll a Wisdom Saving Throw against your own [[Spiritbending DC]] (5) or you gain [[spirit]] (2) [[chaos_energy]] (0).

	- You transform into a creature using [[Spiritbending Slot]] (2). 1 Spiritbending slot = 1 Transformation Point (TP)
