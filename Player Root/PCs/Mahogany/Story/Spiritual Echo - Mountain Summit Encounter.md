
**Context:**  
Immediately after the group's victory over a corrupted spirit. Mahogany, the group's only spirit, has absorbed the entity’s negative energy and purified it from within through a spiritual contest.

---

### 🎯 Mechanics

- **Trigger:** Mahogany completes the spirit cleansing.
    
- **Airbenders in the group** must make a **Perception check (DC 12, with advantage)**.
    

**On Success:**  
They sense a **brief, unnatural shift in air pressure**—a swirl or pulse like a door opening to a hidden chamber, but they are high on a mountain with nothing around. It vanishes after two seconds.

---

### 🌀 Mahogany's Vision

> _(Private: The player can request the others to leave the room for this scene.)_

> As the spiritual remnants fade, Mahogany feels a tingle in the air—not hostile, but watchful.  
> Then, a soft voice, cold as distant wind, whispers directly into her ear:

**"Now... who are you?"**

Mahogany may make a **History or Insight check (DC 17)** to identify the speaker.

---

### 🔍 On Success: Recognizing Rin Kotak


 Mahogany realizes the voice belongs to **[[Rin Kotak]]**, the elusive Spiritualist of the [[Night Bloom]].
 
---

### 🗣️ Mono or Dialogue notes

If she speaks—aloud or in thought—a ripple of wind answers. On a distant cliff edge, so far that no human voice should carry, a **tall, black-robed figure** appears, seated cross-legged, barely more than a wraith in the wind. And yet... his voice is inside her again:

> _“You bent the spirit. Purified it. Took its pain into your own.  
> You’re not like them—not _just_ flesh. Not just bound by the old rules.  
> Intriguing.”_

> _“You stand on a peak, but don’t you feel the cage around you still?  
> The cycle. The Avatar. All of it—wheels within wheels, spinning us endlessly toward the same fate.”_

> _“They call me traitor. But I only want what you just proved is possible—freedom from what _was_. You and I, Mahogany... we could speak again. When the wind is right.”_

The wind sighs. A blink later, the figure is gone. The cliff is bare again.
