
#transformation

You transform into a creature using Spiritbending Slot (20). The Level of creature corresponds with the Bending Slot used.
- Range: e.g. Self (0).
- **Duration:** varies
- Whenever you loose your shapeshifting form involuntarily (by getting reduced to 0 HP in that form) you roll against your own Spiritbending DC (35) or you gain spirit (20) chaos_energy (0).
