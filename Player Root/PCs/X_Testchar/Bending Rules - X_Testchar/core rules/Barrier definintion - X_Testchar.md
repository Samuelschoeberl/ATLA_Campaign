Whenever you suffer unblocked damage for the first Time [[Barrier]] (0) halves (rounded down)
The Second Time you suffer unblocked damage the Barrier is destroyed.

For example when you have 15 [[Barrier]] (0) and suffer 18 damage you suffer 3 Damage and [[Barrier]] (0)  halves to 7. When you then take 11 damage you suffer 4 damage and the [[Barrier]] (0) is destroyed.

Barrier Damage reduction is always applied before any other (e.g. [[General Armor]] (20)) (the barrier also halves/is destroyed if the remaining damage is blocked)
