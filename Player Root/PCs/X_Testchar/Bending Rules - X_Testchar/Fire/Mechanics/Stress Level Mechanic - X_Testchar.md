Firebenders draw power from passion, heat, and intensity. Stress is both a resource and a danger: it fuels more powerful techniques, but too much leaves a bender unstable and at risk of losing control. The following rules convert narrative stress into a simple mechanical resource for firebending moves.

When do you gain [[Player Root/variable/secondary_stat/stress level|stress level]] (0):

- Everytime you take Damage you gain 1 [[stress level]] (0)
- For every [[firebending_slot]] (60) used in a Damaging Move you gain 1 stress level AFTER using the Move
  When you loose [[stress level]] (0):
	- 1 [[stress level]] (0) after every turn
	- ertain moves like [[Smoldering Focus]] (0)
