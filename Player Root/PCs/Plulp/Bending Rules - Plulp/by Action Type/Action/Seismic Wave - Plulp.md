**Seismic Wave**

**Level 5 Slot**
- Create a devastating wave of earth that travels in a line.
- Range: [[Earthbending slot]] (18) * 5 meters long, [[Earthbending slot]] (18) /2 meters wide line
- Damage: [[Earthbending slot]] (18)d6 bludgeoning damage
- Save: Strength Save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (13)

**Effect**:
Send a massive rippling wave through the ground. All creatures in the line must make a Strength save or take full damage, be knocked Prone, and be pushed [[Earthbending slot]] (18) * 2 meters away from you. On a successful save, they take half damage and are not knocked Prone or pushed.
