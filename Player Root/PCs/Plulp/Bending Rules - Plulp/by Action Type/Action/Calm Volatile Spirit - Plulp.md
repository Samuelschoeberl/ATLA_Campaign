#Action_Plulp 

- Range: [[Melee]] (0)

**Effect**:
You enter a Contest of Spiritual energy with a Spirit. 

Roll [[Spiritbending slot]] (3)d20. The Target rolls a Wisdom Saving throw against your [[Spiritbending DC]] (7). On failure your Dice result is getting doubled. If you rolled higher than the spirits current Hitpoints you overpower the Spirit and it calms down and is temporary not hostile. If you roll Lower than the spirits Hitpoints you gain the remaining hitpoints [[chaos_energy]] (0).
