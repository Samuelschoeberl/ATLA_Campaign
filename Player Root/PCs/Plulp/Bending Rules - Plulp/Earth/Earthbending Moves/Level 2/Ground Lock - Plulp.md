
#Bonus_Action_Plulp  

- Range: 2 * [[Earthbending slot]] (18) Meters
- Duration: Until Saved.
- Save: Dex Save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (13).
- Target: 1 * [[Earthbending slot]] (18)  Enemies within range with ground contact
- The Target becomes [[Immobilised]] (0).
- They get to roll a str Save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (13)at the end of their turn.
