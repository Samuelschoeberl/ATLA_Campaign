#Action_Plulp

**Earthen Pit**

**Level 4 Slot**
- Open a pit in the ground beneath enemies.
- Range: 4 * [[Earthbending slot]] (18) meters
- Area: [[Earthbending slot]] (18) meter radius circle
- Depth: [[Earthbending slot]] (18) * 2 meters
- Duration: 1 minute

**Effect**:
All creatures in the area must make a Dexterity save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (13) or fall into the pit, taking [[Earthbending slot]] (18)d6 bludgeoning damage from the fall. Climbing out requires an Athletics check and costs the creature's full [[Movement]] (5).

**Usage**: Excellent battlefield control for separating enemies or creating obstacles.
