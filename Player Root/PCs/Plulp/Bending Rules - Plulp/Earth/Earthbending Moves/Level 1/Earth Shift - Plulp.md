- **Range:** self and every creature within 1+1 * [[Earthbending slot]] (18) meters of you

**Effect**:
Tripping opponents with minor terrain manipulation. The target must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw against your [[Earthbending DC]] (13)) or be knocked Prone.
