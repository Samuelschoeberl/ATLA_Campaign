#Action_Plulp 


- Range: [[Self]] (0)
- **Duration:** varies
	- Whenever you loose your shapeshifting form involuntarily (by getting reduced to 0 HP in that form) you roll a Wisdom Saving Throw against your own [[Spiritbending DC]] (7) or you gain [[spirit]] (3) [[chaos_energy]] (0).
- Effect: 
	- You transform into any learned Shapeshifting form, expending a Spiritbending slot of the form's level.
