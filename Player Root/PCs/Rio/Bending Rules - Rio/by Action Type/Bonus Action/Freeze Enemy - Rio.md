#Bonus_Action_Rio 

- **Range:**  [[Water]] (2) Level meters.

- The waterbender can attempt to freeze a creature that has been hit by a Move that is using [[Water_charge]] (15)(s). It has to succeed a Strength Saving throw against your [[Waterbending DC]] (13) or become [[Paralysed]] (0). 
- The target gets to repeat its save at the start of its turn. The DC drops by 1 every round.
