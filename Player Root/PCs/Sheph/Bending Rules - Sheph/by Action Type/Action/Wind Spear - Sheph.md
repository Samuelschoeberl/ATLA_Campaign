#Action

- **Range:** 5 * Airbending Slot Progression (0) meters.
- **Air Attack Roll (1d20 + 2 + 6)**
- **Damage**: 1d8 bludgeoning per Airbending_slot (4). Deals another 1d6 * Airbending_slot (4) if the Creature hits a Wall.
- The Target has to succeed a Strength (0) saving throw against your Airbending DC (18) or be knocked back half your travelled distance this turn. 
- **Effects:**
	- Utilize airbending to dash towards an enemy within range and line of sight and hit them with a fast blow of pressurised air.
