#Bonus_Action 

- **Range:**  Water (2) Level meters.

- The waterbender can attempt to freeze a creature that has been hit by a Move using Water_charge (15)(s). It has to succeed a Strength Saving throw against your Waterbending DC (17) or become Immobilised (0).
- The target gets to repeat its save at the start of its turn. The DC drops by 1 every round.
