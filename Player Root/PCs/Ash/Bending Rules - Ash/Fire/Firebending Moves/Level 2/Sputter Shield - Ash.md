#Reaction_Ash

- Trigger: You take damage from an attack
- Reduction: You gain [[Firebending_slot]] (6) d4 [[Barrier]] (0)
- Range: [[fire]] (2) level meters 
- Damage: 1d6 fire to the attacker
- **Duration**: Until start of your next turn 

**Effect**:
- A sputtering aura ignites around you; any creature that hits you in melee before your next turn takes 1 fire damage.
