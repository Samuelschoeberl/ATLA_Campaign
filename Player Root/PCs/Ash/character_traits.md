# Who is ash 🔥

## Family

After his mom disappeared, his father blamed their weakness for not being able to save her, so he forced Ash to train countless hours every day (this all happened when he was 8 years old) in hopes that if a tragedy like that happens again, they would be able to save the people they care about. 

Besides being super strict with his training, his father was always very scared of his spirit side, as he believed the spirit world is nothing a human should mingle with, and deemed it obscure.

This made ash train her spirit abilities in secret deep in the forest (his father was a farmer so they lived in a small house in the outskirts of a big fire nation city). During one of these secret trainings, Ash fainted of exhaustion and fell on the water being dragged down stream. When he woke up, he was lying by the shore of the river full of injuries. A small dragon was licking these injuries which seemed to be healing him. Once he recovered his strength, Ash tried going back to his house, and the little dragon followed him, so he decided to keep him (even though he knew his father would feel very strongly against it). That’s how he met his best and only friend.


## ash and the Spirit world 

Ash himself believes the spirit world and his spirit powers not to be evil (as his father tried to convince him of), but rather the key to bringing his mom back. Ash often has been able to communicate with his mom through vivid dreams, which he believes is ways he has to interact with the spirit world. Due to his inexperience, however, he lacks the knowledge of how to use his powers fully, and often he has ended up harming himself. Once he turned 15 he decides to leave his town in search of a master who can teach him how to control his spirit abilities, so one night he just took a bag with food and left home without telling his father. 

Ash’s search is fueled by a mixture of grief and determination, as he firmly believes becoming stronger is the only way he will be able to bring his mom back, which finally will bring him peace.

## Joining the gang

He joins the gang against his initial judgment, after a dream showed him going around with a group of friends. This gave him the idea that joining them would help him find a spirit master easier, and would help him navigate the world, as he doesn’t know anything beyond his small village.
