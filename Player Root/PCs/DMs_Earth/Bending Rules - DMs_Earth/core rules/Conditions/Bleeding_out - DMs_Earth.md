Replaces the standard DnD unconscious state so turns are still taken.

- When you hit 0 [[HP]] (0) you become **Bleeding out** #Bleeding_out_DMs_Earth
- At the start of each of your turns you roll a death saving throw (3 successes = you can stop rolling death saving throws for 3 rounds, 3 failures = dead). You keep taking your turn either way.
- Your total movement is capped at 1 meter per round while Bleeding out.
- Any Move you take while Bleeding out can expend at most 1 bending slot, making your options limited and leaving you vulnerable.
- You cannot take bonus actions while Bleeding out.
