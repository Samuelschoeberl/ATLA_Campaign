
#Bonus_Action_DMs_Earth

- Duration: Until Saved.
- Save: Dex Save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (17).

- The Target becomes [[Immobilised]] (0).
- They get to roll a str Save against your [[Earthbending DC]] (17)at the end of their turn.
