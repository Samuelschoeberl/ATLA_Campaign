#Bonus_Action_Grep 

- Range: 3 * [[Earthbending slot]] (6) Meters
- Duration: 1 round

**Effect**:
Create temporary stone platforms or pillars to elevate yourself or allies. You can create up to [[Earthbending slot]] (6) stone platforms within range, each platform is 1 meter square and can be up to [[Earthbending slot]] (6) meters high. The platforms crumble at the end of your next turn.
