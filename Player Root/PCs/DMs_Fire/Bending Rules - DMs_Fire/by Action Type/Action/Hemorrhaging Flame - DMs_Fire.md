#Action_DMs_Fire

- **Cost**: 10 [[Firebending_slot]] (30)
- **Range:**  Meele

**Effect:** Wreathe the target in needle-fine fire. The target must make a Constitution save versus your [[Firebending DC]] (17). On a failure, they immediately gain the **Bleeding out** condition, even if above 0 HP. On a success, they take minor cauterizing burns but are unaffected by the condition. Reroll at the end of each turn or safe automatically when receiving healing.
