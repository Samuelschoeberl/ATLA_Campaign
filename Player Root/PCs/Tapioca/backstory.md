## Background Story 🍼

My parents found me on a trip of theirs when I was a baby and they told the village that I’m theirs. My parents are very hippie-like, positive people and overly supportive, always telling me I’m special and the other’s just don’t understand it yet.

I live in a very small traditional village **Misty Palms Oasis** that only lives off of tourism, thanks to its one special feature: a glittering oasis with rumored spiritual properties. And also their signature drink ‘Cactus Juice’ which they trade for food and everyone drinks it. (slight hallucinogenic)

I am originally an **Airbender** but don’t know that as my parents and everyone else just assumed I was a **Sandbender**. So I think I’m just very bad at bending. I tried to compensate with combat skills and being invisible.

You were always a little off—too quiet, too bad at bending, too not like the others. People left you alone, which suited you just fine. You found purpose as the **oasis’s cleaner and caretaker**, taking deep pride in polishing the rocks, talking to the water, and protecting its mystery.

Over time, you became deeply devoted to the **Invisible Golden Fish Spirit JinYu**—said to grant clarity and spiritual healing to pilgrims. You fervently believe They are real, even if no one else really does anymore. The elders stopped preaching about Them, the tourists just want drinks, and the village folk roll their eyes at you.

Then one day… the JinYu was stolen!!! You saw some stranger rush away from the oasis. You know the fish isn’t there anymore. The bubbles don't sparkle right. The mist is not misting. The vibe is off. No one believes you. But you’re determined to find Them again—even if it means leaving the only home you've ever known.
