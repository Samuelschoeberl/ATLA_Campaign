#transformation_Tiebeediy

You transform into a creature using [[Spiritbending Slot]] (0). The Level of creature corresponds with the Bending Slot used.
- Range: e.g. [[Self]] (0).
- **Duration:** varies
- Whenever you loose your shapeshifting form involuntarily (by getting reduced to 0 HP in that form) you roll against your own [[Spiritbending DC]] (9) or you gain [[spirit]] (2) [[chaos_energy]] (0).
