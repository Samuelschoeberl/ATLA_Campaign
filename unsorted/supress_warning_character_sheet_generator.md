# Suppress warnings for character sheet generator

List of variable names (stems and display names) that should be exempted from
"unused non-zero" warnings when generating character sheets. These are used
by the sheets or propagated from sheets and can legitimately appear unused in
some contexts.

- environmental_water_charge
- Environmental water charge
- max_hp
- Airbending DC
- Spirit Attack Roll
- Fire Attack Roll
- Water Attack Roll
- Earth Attack Roll
- Water Charges
- Water_Charge
- Water Charge

If you want to add more names, append one per line in the same format.
